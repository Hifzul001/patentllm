# PatentLLM Evaluation Rubric

| Dimension | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Factual accuracy | Major unsupported claims | Mostly accurate with small gaps | Fully grounded in evidence |
| Technical relevance | Generic or off-topic | Relevant but shallow | Deep and claim-aware |
| Evidence use | No patent IDs | Some evidence use | Strong evidence throughout |
| Audience fit | Wrong style | Partly adapted | Clearly tailored |
| Hallucination risk | Invents facts | Minor unsupported inference | Flags uncertainty |
| Actionability | No next steps | General next steps | Concrete business/technical actions |
