# Responsible Use

PatentLLM supports patent intelligence workflows but does not replace patent attorneys, IP counsel, or domain experts.

## Intended use

- patent landscaping
- technology scouting
- competitive intelligence
- internal report drafting
- R&D ideation
- portfolio triage

## Out of scope

- legal opinions
- patentability conclusions
- freedom-to-operate decisions
- infringement analysis
- litigation strategy
- investment decisions without expert review
